# Output: the Label Audit Report

One Word report per product, in the approved format. The marked artwork, the deficiency schedule, the corrected artwork and the suggested label all sit inside the one report; no separate label files are made.

## 1. File, folder and page

- **File:** `[Product]_Label Audit Report.docx`
- **Folder:** `C:\Users\macec\OneDrive\ONE NOTES DOCUMENTS\00_LABEL AUDIT REPORTS\[Client or case]\[NN Product]\`, created without asking (standing path for every label audit report and its artwork).
- **Page:** A4 landscape; margins left 3.5 cm, right 2.8 cm, top and bottom 2.0 cm; Times New Roman 13 pt, 1.5 spacing, justified; tables 11 pt at single spacing.
- **Headings:** Word Heading 1 and Heading 2 styles, maroon (#8B1A1A), auto-numbered by Word (1, 1.1), so the Navigation Pane works. Bold text is never underlined. No em dashes anywhere.
- **Colours:** Critical #D21919, Major #F08200, Minor #E1BE00 (severity shading on the artwork and in the Severity cell); corrections and changed text in blue #0055C8; a remark particular to one item is also set in blue so a reader skimming the standard commentary does not miss it.
- **Header and footer:** "Mace Corporate Associates" right-aligned in the header; "Page X of Y" in the footer. No other marks, no software or AI attribution, and the document properties carry the firm's name only.
- **Language:** plain, human English in house style (`ai-removal-mace-style`); each point made once, in the place where it belongs, and cross-referenced elsewhere.

## 2. Structure of the report

**1. Product identification.** A two-column table: product and brand; marketed by, with address and FSSAI licence; manufactured by, with address and FSSAI licence; pack presentation and net quantity; batch, manufacturing date, expiry and MRP as printed; category as declared; category as it ought to be, with the reason; artwork examined (file name and page); date of audit; law applied (N22 and N16, L&D compendium Version VIII, AC compendium Version IV, LM (PC) Rules as consolidated, and any instrument in force on the audit date).

**2. Executive position.** Six to ten lines: the number of confirmed non-compliances by severity, the single most serious one, and whether the formulation survives the dose reconciliation test. Nothing else.

**3. Marked artwork.** A colour legend (Critical, Major, Minor), then each artwork page as printed, with every problem area shaded in its severity colour and tagged with a number. The number is the Sr. No. in the Deficiency Schedule, so a reader can go from the box to the row and back. Where a mandatory item is missing altogether, a dotted box marks the panel where it should go, with its number. One page of the report per artwork version (carton, strip or foil, sachet, leaflet).

**4. Deficiency Schedule (Part A, label).** One row per number:

| Sr. | As printed | Deficiency | Provision | Severity | Correction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **As printed:** a close-up crop of the area from the artwork, and below it the words as printed, in italics.
- **Deficiency:** stated in the words of the pack, not of the regulation. "The M.R.P. Rs. field stands blank, the figure 456.00 having been overprinted against Mfg. Date" is usable; "MRP non-compliant" is not.
- **Provision:** both texts where the Nutra Regulations are engaged (for example "N22 5(12)(a)(ii)(D); N16 6(3)(iii)(e)"), and the checklist point. Where the two texts differ, the cell says which is stricter.
- **Severity cell** shaded in its colour. **Critical:** a false declaration on the principal display panel (for example a green mark on a non-vegetarian product), a mandatory particular missing altogether, a disease claim, or a composition that cannot lawfully be dosed. **Major:** a mandatory statement absent elsewhere on the pack, or a declaration materially wrong. **Minor:** formatting, placement, font, or an incomplete but not misleading declaration. FSSAI's letter of 17.01.2020 (library 08.2) separates minor defects (improvement notice under s. 32) from mandatory cases (adjudication); where an item is on its minor list, say so.
- **Correction** in blue.
- Column widths (cm): 1.1, 5.6, 6.0, 3.6, 2.0, 5.1.

**5. Composition and rationality (Part B).**
5.1 Ingredient table: Schedule and entry in both texts, permitted range, marker required, marker declared.
5.2 The dose reconciliation table and the intersection (`04_rationality.md`).
5.3 Ingredient-wise evidence under N22 5(7) and N16 3(29).
5.4 Conclusion in one paragraph: dosable, dosable on a corrected usage level, or not dosable at all.

**6. Unable to verify (Open Verification List).**

| Sr. | Point | Provision | Document or check required | From whom |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Recurring entries: certificate of origin for animal-derived ingredients; ingredient specification and certificate of analysis; extract ratio, plant part and marker assay for each botanical; the N16 12(3) product information file for botanical ingredients; the batch manufacturing record for overages; the accredited nutritional analysis report; FoSCoS verification of each licence including category scope and Central licence; the N22 5(7) evidence file; the certificate behind any seal; any prior approval relied on, with its ePAAS reference where applied for on or after 01.06.2026; for a fish oil or omega-3 product, an arsenic test report (inorganic arsenic in fish oils, maximum 0.1 mg/kg) for batches made from 01.12.2026; for an imported product carrying the vegan logo, the Form I certificate from 01.04.2027.

**7. Exposure.** Given once, under four heads, each in its own paragraph, and no finding re-argued here. (a) FSS Act, 2006: misbranding under s. 3(1)(zf) read with ss. 23 and 26, penalty under s. 52; sub-standard food under s. 51 where a composition figure fails on analysis; claims under ss. 24 and 53 with AC18; respective liability of manufacturer and marketer under s. 27, named separately. (b) Drugs and Magic Remedies Act, 1954, where Block R has a hit: the K.S. Saini test applied to the exact words and images; ss. 3, 4 or 5 with s. 7, s. 9 and s. 9A, from the Act in library folder 10 (`01_provisions.md` section 7). (c) Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and the E-Commerce Rules, where Block T was run or the label is itself a misleading advertisement: ss. 2(28), 21, 89 and 84, from `01A_provisions_additions.md` section E. (d) Legal Metrology Act, 2009: s. 18 with s. 36(1) as substituted by the Jan Vishwas Act, 2026 from 01.05.2026 (improvement notice first; then penalty), including e-commerce sales, from `01_provisions.md` section 4. Then the forum map (point 197): which authority each finding goes to.

**8. Recommended course.** Immediate: artwork correction, and stop-supply where a defect is Critical. Short term: reformulation where the reconciliation test failed, licence scope, evidence file. Where the client is on the receiving side, the notice route. Coming changes: any instrument from `01_provisions.md` section 0A that will apply to this label within the next year, with its date.

**9. Corrected artwork.** The same artwork pages, each change boxed in light blue with the corrected wording inside the box in blue, numbered to match the schedule (a moved item gets a second tag such as 1A where it is to go). Square brackets mark figures that must come from the formulation sheet or an accredited analytical report. The designer applies the changes in the source file (AI or CDR); the report does not produce print-ready artwork, because edited vector text would not match the printer's file.

**10. Suggested compliant label.** Panel by panel, in the order the printer uses, with **changed or added text in blue** and unchanged text in black. Every figure that must come from an accredited analytical report is in square brackets; no analytical value is copied from the old label. The recommended usage figure must be a number inside the intersection produced by the dose reconciliation test; where the intersection is empty, no figure is inserted and the label is issued with a covering note that the formulation must change first.

**11. Sources relied on.** Two lists, never blended:
- **Verified:** each provision cited in the report, with the library file and page it was read from; for a provision read from a compendium, the Gazette text behind it as well (principal notification or amendment, file and page, per `01_provisions.md` section 0B), so the schedule can go straight into a notice.
- **Unverified:** anything taken from outside the library (published literature doses in the evidence limb, anything from a document marked UNPINNED in `01_provisions.md`), marked as such. Every statutory row the engine cites is PINNED, so this list is normally confined to literature doses.

## 3. How the report is built

`07_report_builder.py` (in the skill folder and as a Project document) builds the report from the artwork PDF and a findings file in JSON:

- `product`, `client`, `artwork` (path), `pages` (artwork page numbers used), `audit_date`;
- `identification` (list of label and value pairs), `executive` (paragraph);
- `findings`: for each: `n`, `page`, `box` in PDF points `[x0, y0, x1, y1]` (from PyMuPDF `page.search_for()` where the PDF has a text layer; from OCR where it does not, each box then checked by eye on the rendered image), `side` of the tag (`L`, `R`, `T`, `B`), optional `pad` (points added round the box, default 1.5; use 0.6 to 1 where lines of text sit close, so the box does not cut the line above or below), `sev`, `printed`, `deficiency`, `prov`, `fix`, `over` (wording for the corrected artwork), `missing` (true for a dotted box, which goes in a blank part of the panel where the item belongs, never over printed text), optional `extra` boxes (for example 1A, with `mark` true to show it on the marked artwork as well), optional `remark` (set in blue);
- `zoom` (optional): enlarged views of a dense artwork page, each `{page, rect, caption}` with `rect` in PDF points; each view follows the full page in both the marked and the corrected artwork. Use it whenever the artwork is a full carton die-line, where the full page alone is too small to read;
- `online` (optional, Block T): `intro`, `rows` of `{where, shown, defect, sev, fix}`, `screens` of `{path, caption}`; the section prints between the deficiency schedule and the composition section only when rows exist;
- `exposure` may be a list of paragraphs or a dict keyed `FSS Act, 2006`, `Drugs and Magic Remedies Act, 1954`, `Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and E-Commerce Rules`, `Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and Rules`, `Forum map`, each a list of paragraphs (Blocks R and U are recorded here once);
- `composition` (rows for 5.1), `reconciliation` (rows and the conclusion for 5.2 to 5.4), `verification` (rows for section 6), `exposure`, `course`, `label` (panels with runs marked changed or unchanged), `sources_verified`, `sources_unverified`.

Before the report is saved, every page is rendered and looked at: each box sits on the words it marks, no box cuts the line above or below, no tag covers text, no table runs off the page, no page is left blank, and the Navigation Pane shows the numbered headings.

The suggested label changes only what a finding supports. Wording the label already carries in substance (for example "Do not exceed the recommended daily usage", "Keep out of reach of children") is kept as printed, and nothing is added that the label did not say and no finding calls for (no invented dosing time, no new net quantity wording).

## 4. Handover

Where a notice or a representation is to follow, the Deficiency Schedule is the input. Hand it to `notice-reply-skill`, or to Agent 2 of the Nutraceutical Compliance pipeline where that route is in use. This skill does not draft notices, replies or opinions.
