# The checklist: 197 points and 139A, Blocks A to U

Work Blocks A to U in order. Mark each point **NC**, **C**, **UTV** or **NA**, and carry the provision through to the deficiency schedule. The master copy of this list, with the library file, page and Gazette source of every point, is the control ledger `00_CONTROL LEDGER_Nutra Label Audit_25.10.2026.xlsx` in FSSAI LABELLING LIABRARY\FSSAI LABELING-NUTRACEUTICAL ONLY.

**Abbreviations.** **N22** = draft FSS (Nutra) Regulations, 2022, as operated by Directions. **N16** = the 2016 Nutra Regulations as amended in 2021. **L20** = FSS (Labelling and Display) Regulations, 2020. **AC18** = FSS (Advertising and Claims) Regulations, 2018. **LM11** = Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011. **DMR54** = Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954. **ADV dd.mm.yyyy** = the FSSAI direction, order or advisory of that date in library folder 08 (or 06). **CP19** = Consumer Protection Act, 2019. **CPE20** = Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020; **CPE21**, **CPE26** = its Amendment Rules of 2021 and 2026 (CPE26 in force 01.01.2027). **DS21**, **DS23** = Consumer Protection (Direct Selling) Rules, 2021 and their Amendment Rules, 2023. **MA22** = CCPA Guidelines for Prevention of Misleading Advertisements and Endorsements, 2022. **DP23** = CCPA Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 2023. **ITA** = Information Technology Act, 2000. **IT21** = IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021. **PK** = FSS (Packaging) Regulations, 2018. **LMA** = Legal Metrology Act, 2009. **CCPA dd.mm.yyyy** = the CCPA or DoCA advisory of that date in library folder 11. **LMADV** = Legal Metrology Advisory of 08.10.2026 in library folder 09. **FSSAI-EC dd.mm.yyyy** = the FSSAI e-commerce instrument of that date in library folder 11\FSSAI e-commerce. **JV23**, **JV26** = the Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Acts of 2023 and 2026 in library folder 01.

**Three rules for every point.**
1. **Two texts.** Each N22 reference is read with its N16 counterpart in `01_provisions.md` section 2A. Cite both; where they differ, say which is stricter.
2. **In force on the audit date.** Apply only what is in force on the date of the audit (`01_provisions.md` section 0A). The 2026 L&D and Vegan amendments are not applied before 01.07.2027; CPE26 not before 01.01.2027.
3. **Library only.** Every provision is cited from the library file and page given in `01_provisions.md` or `01A_provisions_additions.md`. Every statutory row in this list is PINNED; the only document the list refers to and the library lacks is the FSSAI Order of 22.12.2021, restated in the Order of 05.04.2022 at point 30.


## Block A. Category and classification

1. Is a category declared at all? N22 5(12)(a)(i)(A); N16 6(3)(iii)(a) (HS) or 7(4)(iii)(a) (Nutra).
2. Is the declared category one of the prescribed terms, HEALTH SUPPLEMENT, NUTRACEUTICAL, FOOD FOR SPECIAL DIETARY USE, FOOD FOR SPECIAL MEDICAL PURPOSE, PREBIOTIC FOOD, PROBIOTIC FOOD, or an unprescribed hybrid such as "Nutritional Health Supplement"? N22 5(12)(a)(i)(A).
3. Is it in capital and bold letters? N22 5(12)(a)(i)(A). (N16 asks only for the words.)
4. Is it in the immediate proximity of the name or brand name, on the front of pack? N22 5(12)(a)(i)(A). (N22 only.)
5. Does the composition match the declared category? A Schedule III ingredient, other than an enzyme or protein (Direction of 10.05.2022), on a pack declared a health supplement breaches N22 6(1)(b) and N16 6(2)(i).
6. Where "Dietary supplement" or "Food supplement" is used, is it a permitted substitute for "Health supplement" under N22 6(1)(f)(ii), or a category of its own?
7. Does the product answer the definition in N22 3(e) or 3(g)?
8. Does the product cross into a drug under s. 3(a), (b) or (h) of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940? N22 2(4); N16 6(1)(ii).
9. Does it contain hormones, steroids, narcotic or psychotropic substances, or anything in Schedules E or E-1 of the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945? N22 2(5); N16 3(15).

## Block B. Front of pack

10. Category words present on the front, per point 4. N22 5(12)(a)(i)(A).
11. Target consumer group or age group stated where formulated for a specific group. N22 5(12)(a)(i)(B); N16 3(16) (purpose, target group and duration of use on every label).
12. Veg or non-veg symbol on the principal display panel, close to the name or brand name, on contrast background. L20 5(4)(d).
13. Symbol on a contrasting background. L20 5(4)(d).
14. Symbol at or above the minimum size for the panel area. L20 5(4)(c).
15. Net quantity declared. L20 5(8) read with LM11 r. 6(1)(c).
16. Name of the food on the front, distinct from the brand name. L20 5(1).
17. Font height for the front-of-pack declarations against the panel area table. L20 6.
18. Anything on the front which is itself a claim: a device, a figure, a symbol, a coined element in the brand name. AC18 4(1). The term "100%" in any form (ADV 28.05.2025) and the term "ORS" or anything similar (ADV 08.04.2022, 15.10.2025) are not permitted.

## Block C. Name of the food

19. Is the name of the food declared, and does it reflect the true nature of the food? L20 5(1); N16 6(3)(iii)(b) or 7(4)(iii)(b).
20. Is the brand or coined name carried in addition to, not instead of, the name of the food? L20 5(1).
21. Does the brand name imply a benefit? Test it against AC18 4(1), 4(7) (disclaimer for "natural", "pure" and similar, at the prescribed font size) and 10(1); and N16 4(8)(viii) (implied cure by the name of the product).
22. Is the descriptive name complete: every active, the dosage form, the format? L20 5(1).
23. Where the name emphasises an ingredient, has the ingoing percentage been declared? L20 5(2)(g).
24. Language: English or Hindi in Devanagari, others in addition and not contradicting. L20 4(5).
25. Where a vernacular version of the brand name appears, is it consistent with the English? L20 4(5).

## Block D. List of ingredients

26. Is there a list of ingredients under the heading "Ingredients"? L20 5(2).
27. In descending order of weight or volume at the time of manufacture? L20 5(2). Check against the composition panel; the two frequently contradict each other.
28. Are the excipients and carriers disclosed, or only the actives? L20 5(2).
29. Are additives in the list, by functional class with the specific name or INS number? L20 5(5).
30. Where a compound ingredient is used, are its components declared, and is any animal-origin constituent reflected in the veg mark whatever its percentage? ADV 05.04.2022 (which restates the Order of 22.12.2021, not held).
31. Is added water declared where applicable? L20 5(2).
32. Flavours declared as natural, nature identical or artificial? N22 5(4)(c), reg. 3.3.1 of the FSS (FPS and FA) Regulations, 2011.
33. Do the ingredient list and the composition panel name the same substances? Discrepancies are separately actionable. L20 4(3).
34. Is the botanical name given for each botanical, with the part used? ADV 15.10.2026, para 6; N22 5(2)(a) footnote; N16 7(3)(v).

## Block E. Composition against the Schedules

35. For each ingredient, identify the Schedule and entry in both texts: the complete tables are in `02A_schedules_full.md` (Schedule II, 439 botanicals with part and range; Schedule III Part B, 192 entries; Schedule IV), and the structural rules, Schedule I and Schedule III Part A in `02_schedules.md`. An ingredient in no Schedule is non-specified and needs approval under N22 5(2)(d) and the NSF&FI Regulations, 2017 (applications only on ePAAS since 01.06.2026, ADV 06.05.2026); the finding is UTV until the approval is produced. Check the banned and restricted list: PABA is banned (ADV 16.07.2020); guarana only as isolated caffeine, a standardised guarana extract needs approval (ADV 11.01.2022); probiotic strains may carry the old or new name (ADV 13.01.2022).
36. Schedule I nutrients: is the permitted chemical source declared, not merely the nutrient name? N22 Sch. I; N16 Sch. I.
37. Schedule I nutrients: within the permitted maximum and not above one RDA? N22 6(1)(c)(i) or 6(2)(c)(ii); N16 3(4), 6(2)(iii), 7(2)(iii). A product above one RDA is not even approvable as a food (ADV 11.05.2023).
38. Where a nutrient content claim is made, at least 15 per cent of RDA, or 30 per cent for a higher content claim? N22 6(1)(c)(i); N16 3(5).
39. Overages: is "appropriate overages added" declared in the composition panel, and is the overage within Table C? N22 Sch. I Note 2 and Table C (p. 23).
40. Schedule II botanicals: is the part used the part listed in the Schedule? N22 5(2)(a) footnote; N16 7(3)(v). Ashwagandha: root or root extract only; leaf, whole plant or unstated part is NC (ADV 16.04.2026).
41. Schedule II botanicals: is the extract ratio declared, and does the quantity reconcile with the raw-herb range once the ratio is applied? N22 5(2)(a) footnote.
42. Schedule II botanicals: within the range for the applicable age group, half for 5 to 16 years, one quarter for 2 to 5 years? N22 Sch. II Notes 4 and 5.
43. Schedule III Part A: within the range, floor and ceiling? N22 6(2)(c)(i); N16 7(3)(i).
44. Schedule III Part A: standardised to the marker the Schedule prescribes, and is that standardisation declared? N22 6(2)(c)(i); N16 7(3)(i).
45. Schedule III Part B: no range, so has the client's scientific justification been called for? N22 6(2)(c)(i) second limb; N16 7(3)(iii) and (iv).
46. Any single purified chemical entity sold without prior approval, outside the exceptions? N22 5(6) (excepts botanical extracts, vitamins, minerals, amino acids, nucleotides); N16 3(30) (excepts only botanical extracts). State which text is stricter.
47. Extraction solvent food grade, and declared where relevant? N22 5(9)(a); N16 3(32) (water, ethyl alcohol or hydro-alcoholic only).
48. Any ingredient with a Schedule caution ("not recommended during pregnancy", "for children below 5 years" and the like), and does the label carry it? N22 5(12)(a)(ii)(F); N16 6(3)(iii)(g) or 7(4)(iii)(h); ADV 15.10.2026, Annexure I (table in 02_schedules.md section 3; every caution printed in the range column of 02A_schedules_full.md).

## Block F. Combination rationale

49. Is each ingredient present at a quantity for which scientific evidence exists, or at a token quantity? N22 5(7); N16 3(29).
50. Run the dose reconciliation test in 04_rationality.md. Is there any single daily dose at which every ingredient sits inside its permitted range? N22 5(7); N16 3(29).
51. Where the recommended usage is not stated, no such test is possible. That is itself the finding, and it compounds the Block L defect. N22 5(12)(a)(ii)(B).
52. Any ingredient below the floor of its Schedule III Part A range at the stated dose? N22 6(2)(c)(i); N16 7(3)(i).
53. Any ingredient above the ceiling at the stated dose? N22 6(2)(c)(i); N16 7(3)(i).
54. Two ingredients duplicating the same function without justification? N22 5(7); N16 3(29).
55. Has the client been asked, in writing, for the N22 5(7) and N16 3(29) evidence, and, for botanicals, the N16 12(3) product information file? Record the date of the request.

56. Is a nutritional information panel present? L20 5(3). The exemption in L20 5(3)(c)(xiii), Version VIII, covers FSDU and FSMP, not health supplements or nutraceuticals; the tablet and capsule exemption for HS and Nutra comes only on 01.07.2027.
57. Energy in kcal. L20 5(3)(b)(i).
58. Protein in g. L20 5(3)(b)(ii)(A).
59. Carbohydrate in g. L20 5(3)(b)(ii)(B).
60. Total sugars in g. L20 5(3)(b)(ii)(B).
61. Added sugars in g. L20 5(3)(b)(ii)(B).
62. Total fat in g. L20 5(3)(b)(ii)(C).
63. Saturated fat in g, where total fat exceeds 0.5 per cent in the final food (L20 5(3)(b)(ii)(C), first proviso, LD p. 10).
64. Trans fat in g, on the same condition. L20 5(3)(b)(ii)(C), first proviso.
65. Cholesterol in mg, only where the product contains fats of animal origin and total fat exceeds 0.5 per cent (L20 5(3)(b)(ii)(C), second proviso, LD p. 10).
66. Sodium in mg. Check specifically where a sodium salt is an active (sodium hyaluronate, sodium ascorbate). L20 5(3)(b)(ii)(D).
67. Values per 100 g or 100 ml or per single consumption pack? L20 5(3)(a).
68. Amount of food in g or ml beside the serving measure? L20 5(3)(b)(iv).
69. Number of servings in the pack? L20 5(3)(b)(iv).
70. Vitamins and minerals in metric units? L20 5(3)(b)(iii). (A vitamin D figure in IU alone fails.)

## Block H. RDA and serving declarations

71. Per serve percentage contribution to RDA declared? L20 5(3)(b).
72. On the prescribed basis of 2,000 kcal, 67 g total fat, 22 g saturated fat, 2 g trans fat, 50 g added sugar, 2,000 mg sodium; or, for a product aimed at a specific age group or condition, the respective RDA (ADV 03.02.2022)? L20 5(3)(b).
73. Per cent RDA of each nutrient declared? N22 5(12)(a)(ii)(I); N16 6(3)(iii)(e) or 7(4)(iii)(d).
74. Is the ICMR-NIN 2020 RDA the basis (ADV 16.07.2021 and 02.08.2021, in force from 01.01.2024)?
75. Recompute every declared percentage. Does it reconcile, and does every row use the same basis? The standard reference is the RDA for men and women in sedentary work (ADV 02.08.2021, Annexure IB Note 1, p. 3; table in 02_schedules.md); a label that mixes the men's figure in one row with the women's in another is inconsistent.
76. Where no RDA exists, "Daily value not established" or equivalent, not a blank? N22 5(12)(a)(ii)(I).
77. Serving size defined, and the same figure the composition panel and the usage instruction assume? L20 5(3)(b)(iv).
78. Amount of the nutrients or physiologically active substances on pack or leaflet? N22 5(12)(a)(iii)(A); N16 6(3)(iii)(c) or 7(4)(iii)(c).

120. Any implied claim by picture, vignette, device or symbol. N16 4(8)(viii); AC18 4(1).

## Block R. Drugs and Magic Remedies Act

This block runs in parallel to Block M. The same disease claim that fails AC18 10 and N22 2(6) is also a DMR54 offence, and DMR54 is penal. The Act is in library folder 10 (India Code text as on 15.04.2026; sections at pp. 4 to 8, Schedule of 54 conditions at pp. 9 to 11), so the statutory rows are PINNED (01_provisions.md section 7). Neither Jan Vishwas Act touches this Act (01A section C), so the s. 7 penalty stands as printed.

157. Treat every panel, insert, leaflet, sticker and outer carton as an "advertisement". DMR54 s. 2(a).
158. Is the product a "drug" for DMR54 at all? In s. 2(b) the words "other than food" sit in the structure-or-function limb, s. 2(b)(iii), only; the disease limb, s. 2(b)(ii), carries no food exclusion. DMR54 s. 2(b).
159. Words, device or image suggesting use for miscarriage or prevention of conception. DMR54 s. 3(a).
160. Capacity for sexual pleasure. Watch "vigour", "vitality", "stamina", "performance", "men's wellness", "libido", and the imagery. DMR54 s. 3(b).
